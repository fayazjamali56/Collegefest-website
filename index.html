About us

Built for people who watch every ball

GreenStump started as a simple idea: cricket moves fast, and the scoreboard should move with it. We bring live scores, full scorecards, rankings and news together into one clean feed, so you're never juggling five tabs during a run chase.

Everything on this page updates automatically from live data feeds — no manual refreshing, no stale scores. Whether it's a T20 chase going to the last over or a Test match grinding through day four, GreenStump keeps you at the crease.

Live, automated updates

Scores, wickets and overs refresh straight from the data feed as play happens.

Full rankings, every format
